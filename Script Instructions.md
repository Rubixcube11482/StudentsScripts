• Alt account (combat):

• Handles all combat.
• Good melee stats (>50 for low-level NPC, >70 for high-level NPC).
• Decent melee weapon equipped.
• No armor (to make killing easier for the main).
• Must carry dwarven rock cake or locator orb.
• *Salve amulet works on ghosts.*
• Optional: configure combat prayers (e.g., Piety). Script will maintain them.

• Main account (zeal):

• Will finish the kill.
• Needs a decent melee weapon.

3. GUI Settings (optional):

• Use 4 frag NPCs: Targets NPCs with 4 frags instead of 2.
• Alt account is high level: Enable if alt consistently gets >24 frags. Increases zeal/hr, but not worth it if not at frag soft cap.

Script Requirements & Notes

Recommended setup without Dust battlestaff:
• Payment: air runes

• Staff of earth
• Raiments of the Eye robes
• Graceful gloves and cape
• Your best defensive amulet and shield

Bank setup
There are two options for using deposit all
You can either:
1. Remove placeholders for your colossal pouch, rune pouch and your payment rune and then fill your bank with bank fillers. Please note that doing this and receiving a rune from ZMI that you don't have will cause issues with the script, make sure you have 1 of each rune you can receive from ZMI first
2. You can use deposit lock on specific slots which hold your colossal pouch, payment rune and rune pouch. Please keep in mind that the script will move your colossal pouch and rune pouch on startup

First time running instructions
• Ensure you have your bank set up as discussed above
• If using deposit lock, run the script and let it move your items, then stop the script and lock the slots the script assigned you (it is different for every person, which is why I recommend you run it once to establish your inventory)

Hardcore Mode

When enabled in the GUI, Hardcore Mode provides extra safety:

• Uses rings from inventory, allowing a Ring of Life to remain equipped.
• Monitors hitpoints and teleports to Ferox if too low (default: 25 HP; configurable at line 752).
• Note: this does not guarantee account safety.

Examples:
• Mort Myre Fungus: Ghasts hit up to 3. With 35+ HP, a Ring of Life ensures safety.
• Zamorak Wines: Monks hit up to 8. With 85+ HP, a Ring of Life ensures safety. Otherwise, babysit or await teleport/combat-detection updates.
• Red Spider Eggs: Spidines hit up to 5. With 55+ HP, a Ring of Life ensures safety.
